Who We Are

Research with people at the centre.

Our Gender Lens was founded with a clear objective: to apply intersectional research to complex policy issues, ensuring that marginalized voices are central to decision-making processes.

02
Our Mission

Bridging research and action.

We aim to bridge the gap between academic research and practical policy implementation. By providing accessible explainers, rigorous policy briefs, and actionable guidelines, we empower organizations and governments to create more equitable environments.

Our Approach

There is more than one way to see a story.

Every project we undertake is viewed through a multifaceted lens that considers gender, race, class, and ability.

This intersectional framework is essential for understanding the true impact of policies on diverse communities.
