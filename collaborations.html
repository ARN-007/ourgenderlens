COLLABORATIONS

Building Change Together.

We believe meaningful change happens when different perspectives, experiences and areas of expertise come together.

Our collaborations bring together people, organizations and institutions committed to creating more inclusive and equitable futures.

WORKING TOGETHER

Different perspectives. Shared purpose.

Our Gender Lens works with individuals, institutions, researchers, organizations and communities to explore gender through thoughtful research, dialogue and action.

These partnerships allow us to exchange knowledge, challenge assumptions and create spaces where diverse perspectives can shape better ideas and outcomes.
